The Beauty Times

Sephora and chatbots

Chatbots & voice assistants at Sephora: an investigation and a strategy.

8students
16weeks of planning
200test customers
4strategic pillars

Context

Group project (8 students) at ENCG Settat, supervised by Ms. Doulkaid Amal. The final presentation was staged as an investigation, “Sephora Secret”, opened by an intro video.

The question

“How can Sephora use its chatbot to deliver a more personalized, interactive experience and build customer loyalty?”

The verdict

Sephora is ahead in visual BeautyTech (Virtual Artist, augmented reality, omnichannel) but behind in voice commerce.

Our strategy

Turn the chatbot into a true smart beauty assistant, by text and by voice, built on 4 pillars:

  1. 01Personalized expertise Skin diagnosis, tailored routines.
  2. 02Interactive experience Quizzes, mini-challenges.
  3. 03Omnichannel Website, app, store.
  4. 04Voice An assistant that answers out loud.

“Your new tailor-made beauty expert: 24/7, 100% you.”

A 16-week launch plan, a test panel of 200 customers, and an estimated marketing budget of MAD 32,000–44,000.

Confidential

Our creative idea

GlowTalk by Sephora — A shopping-mall pop-up: an instant skin diagnosis, a chat with the chatbot on an interactive mirror, then a small printed recap, a matching sample and a QR code to continue the conversation in the app.

Sephora Voice Expert — A voice assistant in the app, on the website and in store: “Add a waterproof mascara to my cart.”
